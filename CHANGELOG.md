# OpenLogi Batteries Changelog

## [Initial Version]

- Show the lowest connected OpenLogi device battery in the macOS menu bar.
- List online devices with battery and connection details.
- Support Bolt, Unifying, Lightspeed, Bluetooth, wired USB, and unknown direct connections.
- Indicate charging devices in the list and menu bar.
- Discover the OpenLogi CLI automatically or use a configured executable path.
- Quit and suspend the menu-bar item until the command is manually reactivated.
